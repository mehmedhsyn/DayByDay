DayByDay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

DayByDay (“the app”) is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: your habit data stays on your device, we run no servers that collect it, and we do not show ads or use analytics.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • Your habits, their schedules and reminder times
  • Your daily completion history, streaks, mood entries and short daily notes
  • The name you optionally enter, and your settings (language, dark mode)

This data is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device. Depending on your phone’s settings, Android’s own device backup may include this data in your Google account; that is controlled by Android and your device settings, not by us.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to use backup, you sign in with your Google account and allow the app to use the Google Drive “app data” permission (drive.appdata). The app then saves a backup file of your habit data in a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive, and can restore it later. This folder is not visible in your normal Drive files, and the developer has no access to it or to your Google account content. The app does not read any other files in your Drive.

You can remove the backup and revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps & services) and in Google Drive settings (Manage apps → Delete hidden app data).

3. Purchases

The Pro upgrade (subscription or one-time purchase) is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro is active on your account. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

4. Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: used for the habit reminders you set. They are created locally on your device.
  • Receive boot completed: used only to re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
  • Vibrate: used for feedback when you complete a habit.
  • Internet: used only for Google Drive backup and Google Play purchases.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

6. Third-party services

The app relies on Google Play Billing and, only if you use backup, Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. These are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention and your rights

Because your data lives on your device (and, if you enable backup, in your own Google Drive), you control it. You can delete it by clearing the app’s data, uninstalling the app, and deleting the hidden backup as described above. If you are in the EU/EEA or another region with data-protection rights, you may contact us with any questions about your data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on its Google Play page.
