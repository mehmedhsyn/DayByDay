DayByDay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026
DayByDay (“the app”) is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: your habit data stays on your device, we run no external servers that collect it, and we do not show ads or use tracking analytics.

1. Information stored on your device

To function properly, the app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your habits, their schedules, and reminder times
  • Your daily completion history, streaks, mood entries, and short daily notes
  • The display name you optionally enter, and your settings (language, dark mode)

This data is never transmitted to the developer's servers. Uninstalling the app permanently removes this data from your device. Depending on your phone’s system settings, Android’s automatic system backup may include this local app data in your personal Google account storage; that process is managed entirely by Android and your device settings, not by us.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to enable the backup feature, you sign in using your Google Account. During this step, your Google account ID/email is processed temporarily by Google Sign-In solely to authenticate your identity. We do not collect, store, or view your account credentials.

With your consent, the app requests access to the restricted Google Drive "app data" scope (drive.appdata). The app creates and updates a single backup file of your habit data inside a hidden, app-specific directory within your personal Google Drive account. This directory is inaccessible to other applications and invisible in your normal Drive file list. The developer has zero access to your backup files or Google account contents. The app does not read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

You can revoke the app's access and delete stored backup data at any time via your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps & services) or directly in Google Drive settings (Manage apps → Delete hidden app data).

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (such as the Pro upgrade via subscription or one-time payment) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial details. The app only queries Google Play Billing to verify whether an active Pro license is associated with your account. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime via Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

4. System permissions and notifications

  • Notifications: Used exclusively to deliver the local habit reminders you configure. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Receive boot completed: Used solely to re-schedule your local reminders after your device restarts or when the system time zone changes.
  • Vibrate: Used to provide haptic feedback when completing a habit.
  • Internet: Used strictly for communicating with Google Play Billing to verify purchases and, if enabled by you, transferring backup data directly to your personal Google Drive storage.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not integrate third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone data, or unique hardware identifiers.

6. Third-party services

The app relies on Google Play Billing and, conditionally (only if you choose to use the optional backup feature), Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. These services operate under Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention and user rights

Because your data resides locally on your device (and inside your personal Google Drive, if backup is enabled), you retain full control over it. You can permanently delete your data by clearing the app’s data in Android settings, uninstalling the app, or removing the hidden app data from your Google Drive account. If you reside in the EU/EEA or another region with applicable data protection laws, you may contact us with any inquiries regarding your data.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. Material changes will be communicated within the app or on its Google Play Store listing page.
